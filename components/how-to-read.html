How to read this book

You don't have to read it in order.

Part 01 is about decisions: do you need a design system, what's it worth, what does it cost, and how do you run it? Part 02 is about the foundations everything is built on. Part 03 is about components: the buttons, fields and tables people use every day. Pick the route that fits your job.

Things you'll see along the way

Scenarios

Grey panels tell a real-world situation in three beats: what was happening, what went wrong, and what a design system changes. They're composites of what we see in practice, not specific clients.

New words

When we introduce a term for the first time, you'll find a short plain-English definition in the margin beside it. You never need to know the jargon in advance.

The short version

Every chapter ends with a dark summary box of three or four things to remember, so you can skim a chapter's conclusions in thirty seconds.
