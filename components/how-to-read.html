How to read this book

You don't have to read it in order.

Part 01 is about decisions: do you need a design system, what's it worth, what does it cost, and how do you run it? Part 02 covers the foundations everything is built on. Part 03 introduces components, and Part 04 goes deep into how they're structured, configured and coded. Part 05 covers patterns, content, scaling and tools. Part 06 is about operating the system inside an organisation: teams, rhythms, enablement, communication, requests, governance and measures, ending with a twelve-month plan. Pick the route that fits your job.

Things you'll see along the way

Scenarios

Grey panels tell a real-world situation in three beats: what was happening, what went wrong, and what a design system changes. They're composites of what we see in practice, not specific clients.

New words

When we introduce a term for the first time, you'll find a short plain-English definition in a "New word" band right beside it. You never need to know the jargon in advance.

The short version

Every chapter ends with a dark summary box of three or four things to remember, so you can skim a chapter's conclusions in thirty seconds.
