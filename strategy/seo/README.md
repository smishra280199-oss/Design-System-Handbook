# Layer One — Design systems blog strategy

**Status:** proposal for review · **Prepared:** 9 November 2026 · **Source material:** *The Design Systems Playbook*, edition 1.1

A plan to turn the playbook into a search-friendly knowledge hub at `layerone.co.in/writing/` that
teaches first and positions Layer One as the people who know how design systems are *run*, not only built.
No articles have been written yet: this is the architecture and roadmap for review.

## Key findings

1. **The reference site's strength is structure, not search targeting.** DESK uses categories with counts, numbered series and clean schema, but essay-style titles. Layer One should borrow the structure and use search-matched titles.
2. **Layer One's existing writing mostly lives on Medium**, so the authority goes to Medium. Move it on-site, under one `/writing/` folder.
3. **Search results are dominated by vendor blogs** (UXPin, Supernova, Frontify, zeroheight) that are broad but generic, and by Medium posts that rank on domain strength.
4. **The clearest gaps match the playbook's strengths:** operating models by company size, team org charts, an honest ROI model, computed colour and contrast data, and copyable governance templates.
